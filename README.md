# 🌍 Global Health Insights Dashboard | Tableau Project

## 📝 Overview

This project was completed during my **Data Technician Bootcamp** and focuses on analysing global health data using **Tableau**. Utilizing the Gapminder Health dataset, I created an interactive dashboard to explore health trends, population demographics, and regional disparities across countries and continents.

The resulting dashboard enables users to investigate key health metrics—such as life expectancy, BMI, population growth, and disease-related indicators—through clear visualisations and interactive filters.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

✅ Analyse global health trends across countries and continents

✅ Compare life expectancy across different geographic regions

✅ Explore relationships between health indicators and population metrics

✅ Visualise changes in health outcomes over time

✅ Communicate findings through effective data storytelling and dashboard design

I built a suite of targeted worksheets in Tableau to evaluate different facets of global health outcomes before consolidating them into an interactive dashboard layout.

## 🚀 Key Findings

Through this analysis, I uncovered several key business and global health insights:

- **Increasing Longevity:** Global life expectancy has consistently grown over time, reaching ~82 years in top-performing nations.
- **Regional Disparities:** Europe and North America exhibit the highest average life expectancy levels globally.
- **Health Correlations:** Higher BMI levels show a positive correlation with life expectancy in developed regions, reflecting access to healthcare and nutrition.
- **Demographic Shifts:** Gender distributions vary significantly by region and impact overall population growth dynamics.
